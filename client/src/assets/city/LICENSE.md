# City textures

All files in this folder are CC0 (public domain).

- `sky.hdr`: Poly Haven, "Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky)", 1k.
- `asphalt*`, `brick*`, `concrete*`, `stone*`, `glass-*`, `grass*`: ambientCG (Asphalt012, Facade018A, Facade019A,
  Facade020A, Facade001, Facade006, Grass004), resized and packed (`-orm` = occlusion, roughness, metalness).
